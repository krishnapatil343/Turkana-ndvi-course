# North Turkana NDVI practical

Allow 6–8 hours after Earth Engine access is working. First read [What is NDVI?](01_What_is_NDVI.md) and complete the [entry check](02_Entry_Check.md). Then follow the [Earth Engine practical notebook](Earth_Engine_Practical.ipynb) and run the [complete JavaScript](North_Turkana_NDVI_Earth_Engine.js) in the Earth Engine Code Editor.

You will choose a North Turkana rangeland site, inspect rainfall and NDVI charts, calculate lag correlations, and export two CSVs to Google Drive. The notebook lists what to hand in.
