### 1 Why does green vegetation often give a high NDVI?

Explain what happens to red and near-infrared light at a green leaf.

<details>
<summary>Show feedback</summary>

Chlorophyll absorbs much of the red light. The leaf's internal structure reflects more near-infrared light. This gives a positive contrast between NIR and red reflectance.

</details>

### 2 What does this pixel tell us?

A pixel has NIR reflectance of 0.30 and red reflectance of 0.20. Calculate NDVI. Name one site or data check you would make before interpreting that value as vegetation stress.

<details>
<summary>Show feedback</summary>

NDVI = (0.30 − 0.20) / (0.30 + 0.20) = **0.20**. For example, check whether the pixel includes bare soil or water, whether it passed quality screening, or whether 0.20 is typical for this site and season.

</details>
